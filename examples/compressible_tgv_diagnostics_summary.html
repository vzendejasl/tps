Compressible TGV Diagnostics Summary

This note summarizes the compressible Taylor-Green vortex diagnostics written by writeTGVDiagnostics() in src/M2ulPhyS.cpp. It documents the grouped CSV output and the exact mathematical meaning of each quantity.

Output Files

The diagnostics are split across four CSV files written next to the configured summary filename.

tgv_diagnostics.csv
tgv_diagnostics_budget.csv
tgv_diagnostics_integrals.csv
tgv_diagnostics_extrema.csv

All values are written in scientific notation with 16 digits after the decimal point.

Time-Based Stop and Output Control

The compressible TGV path now supports optional time-based stopping and optional time-based restart and visualization scheduling.

  • time/stopMode = cycles | time | either
  • time/finalTime
  • io/outputMode = cycles | time | either
  • io/outputIntervalTime

When stopMode = time or either, the solver clips the final step so that \( t = t_{\mathrm{final}} \) is reached exactly.

When outputMode = time or either, restart and visualization writes are triggered when the running time crosses the next scheduled output time. The output events are therefore time-based, but the VisIt datasets are still labeled by the current cycle number through the solver iteration count.

The visualization history is retained as multiple visit_*.mfem_root outputs, while the restart file is currently a rolling checkpoint named restart_<outdirBase>.sol*.h5. Each scheduled restart write overwrites the previous restart file.

Example: the input deck examples/input.compressible_tgv_re400_m125_32cubed_time_output.ini uses \( t_{\mathrm{final}} = 3.1831 \) and \( \Delta t_{\mathrm{out}} = 3.1831 / 26 \) to produce 25 interior output events, plus the initial write at \( t = 0 \), plus the final write at \( t = 3.1831 \).

Pointwise Definitions

The diagnostics are evaluated from the primitive and conserved fields reconstructed at quadrature points:

  • \( \rho \): density
  • \( \mathbf{u} = (u, v, w) \): velocity
  • \( \rho E \): conserved total energy density
  • \( T \): temperature
  • \( p = R \rho T \): pressure
  • \( c = \sqrt{\gamma R T} \): sound speed
  • \( M = |\mathbf{u}| / c \): local Mach number
  • \( M_t = \sqrt{\langle M^2 \rangle / 3} \): turbulent Mach number used for the zero-mean TGV field
  • \( \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} = \partial u/\partial x + \partial v/\partial y + \partial w/\partial z \): velocity divergence
  • \( \boldsymbol{\omega} = \nabla \times \mathbf{u} \): vorticity
  • \( |\boldsymbol{\omega}|^2 = \omega_x^2 + \omega_y^2 + \omega_z^2 \)
  • \( S_{ij} = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\partial u_i/\partial x_j + \partial u_j/\partial x_i\right) \): symmetric strain tensor
  • \( S^2 = \sum_{i,j} S_{ij} S_{ij} \)
  • \( \mu(T) \): viscosity from the current TGV transport model
  • \( \mu(T)/\mu_0 \): viscosity ratio used in the diagnostics
  • \( \rho e = \rho E - \tfrac{1}{2}\rho |\mathbf{u}|^2 \): internal energy density
  • \( \tau:\nabla \mathbf{u} = 2\mu S^2 - \tfrac{2}{3}\mu (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u})^2 \): viscous dissipation density used here

Global Scaling

The code uses the physical domain volume \( |\Omega| \) and the TGV reference quantities \( \rho_0 \), \( U_0 \), \( L \), \( \mathrm{Re} \), and \( \mu_0 \).

  • Energy normalization: \( \rho_0 U_0^2 |\Omega| \)
  • Budget normalization: \( \rho_0 U_0^3 |\Omega| / L \)
  • Dissipation normalization: \( \mathrm{Re}\, U_0^2 |\Omega| / L^2 \)

Summary File

tgv_diagnostics.csv contains the main normalized diagnostics.

Column Definition Meaning
time \( t \) Physical simulation time.
iter \( n \) Time-step index.
kinetic_energy \( \dfrac{1}{2 \rho_0 U_0^2 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} \rho |\mathbf{u}|^2 \, d\Omega \) Normalized volume-averaged kinetic energy.
internal_energy \( \dfrac{1}{\rho_0 U_0^2 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} \rho e \, d\Omega \) Normalized internal energy.
total_energy \( \dfrac{1}{\rho_0 U_0^2 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} \rho E \, d\Omega \) Normalized conserved total energy. In this dry-air model, this is internal plus kinetic energy.
turbulent_mach \( \sqrt{\dfrac{1}{3 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega}\left(\dfrac{|\mathbf{u}|}{c}\right)^2 \, d\Omega} \) Rms turbulent Mach number. For the Taylor-Green vortex, the mean velocity is zero, so the resolved velocity is used as the fluctuation velocity.
solenoidal_dissipation \( \dfrac{L^2}{\mathrm{Re}\, U_0^2 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} \dfrac{\mu}{\mu_0} |\boldsymbol{\omega}|^2 \, d\Omega \) Rotational contribution based on vorticity magnitude.
dilatational_dissipation \( \dfrac{4 L^2}{3 \mathrm{Re}\, U_0^2 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} \dfrac{\mu}{\mu_0} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u})^2 \, d\Omega \) Compressive contribution based on velocity divergence.
enstrophy \( \dfrac{1}{2 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} |\boldsymbol{\omega}|^2 \, d\Omega \) Volume-averaged enstrophy.

Budget File

tgv_diagnostics_budget.csv contains normalized budget terms.

Column Definition Meaning
pressure_work \( \dfrac{L}{\rho_0 U_0^3 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} p \, \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} \, d\Omega \) Pressure-dilatation contribution.
viscous_dissipation \( \dfrac{L}{\rho_0 U_0^3 |\Omega|}\int_{\Omega} \tau : \nabla \mathbf{u} \, d\Omega \) Normalized viscous dissipation based on the code's stress contraction.
viscous_work \( -\,\text{viscous\_dissipation} \) The current implementation reports viscous work as the negative of the dissipation term.

Integrals File

tgv_diagnostics_integrals.csv contains the unnormalized integrated quantities.

Column Definition Meaning
ke_integral \( \int_{\Omega} \rho |\mathbf{u}|^2 \, d\Omega \) Integral of the kinetic-energy numerator used by kinetic_energy.
internal_energy_integral \( \int_{\Omega} \rho e \, d\Omega \) Integral of internal energy before normalization.
total_energy_integral \( \int_{\Omega} \rho E \, d\Omega \) Integral of the conserved total energy.
vorticity_integral \( \int_{\Omega} |\boldsymbol{\omega}|^2 \, d\Omega \) Unweighted vorticity-squared integral.
weighted_vorticity_integral \( \int_{\Omega} \dfrac{\mu}{\mu_0} |\boldsymbol{\omega}|^2 \, d\Omega \) Viscosity-weighted vorticity integral used in the solenoidal dissipation diagnostic.
divergence_integral \( \int_{\Omega} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u})^2 \, d\Omega \) Unweighted divergence-squared integral.
weighted_divergence_integral \( \int_{\Omega} \dfrac{\mu}{\mu_0} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u})^2 \, d\Omega \) Viscosity-weighted divergence integral used in the dilatational dissipation diagnostic.
pressure_dilatation_integral \( \int_{\Omega} p \, \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} \, d\Omega \) Unnormalized pressure-dilatation budget term.
viscous_dissipation_integral \( \int_{\Omega} \tau : \nabla \mathbf{u} \, d\Omega \) Unnormalized viscous dissipation budget term.

Extrema File

tgv_diagnostics_extrema.csv contains extrema and monitoring quantities.

Column Definition Meaning
min_rho \( \min_{\mathbf{x} \in \Omega} \rho(\mathbf{x}) \) Minimum density over the domain.
min_pressure \( \min_{\mathbf{x} \in \Omega} p(\mathbf{x}) \) Minimum pressure over the domain.
max_mach \( \max_{\mathbf{x} \in \Omega} |\mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x})| / c(\mathbf{x}) \) Maximum pointwise Mach number.
max_abs_divu \( \max_{\mathbf{x} \in \Omega} |\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x})| \) Maximum absolute divergence over the domain.

Conservation Note

For the dry-air compressible TGV in a periodic box with no forcing, the global conserved energy is \( \int_{\Omega} \rho E \, d\Omega \), represented by total_energy_integral and its normalized form total_energy.

kinetic_energy and internal_energy are not separately conserved; viscous effects transfer energy between them.
